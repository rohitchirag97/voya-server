    How to play

    Bet before launch. The rocket takes off and the multiplier climbs. Cash out any time to win your bet × the multiplier. If the rocket crashes before you cash out, that bet is lost.

    1. 1

      Bet before launch

      Set an amount and tap Bet during the countdown. Tap again to add the same amount again; the panel shows your total.

    2. 2

      Watch it climb

      The multiplier grows the longer the rocket flies: 2x after about 10 seconds, 10x after about 33.

    3. 3

      Cash out in time

      Each panel's button shows what it would pay right now. Tap Cash out and it goes straight into your coins.

    4. 4

      Or it crashes

      Anything still in when the rocket crashes is lost. A new round starts a few seconds later.

    Two bets at once

    You have two bet panels. A common way to play: Bet 1 with auto cash out at a safe 1.50x to lock in a win, and Bet 2 left running for a bigger multiplier. Each panel cashes out on its own.

    Auto cash out

    Switch on Auto and set a target. If the rocket reaches it, that panel cashes out at exactly that multiplier, even if you look away.

    Fair by design

    • The crash point is decided before anyone bets and never depends on bets.
    • Whatever you aim for, you get back about 98% of what you bet on average; about 2 rounds in 100 crash right at 1.00x. No strategy beats it. Play for fun.
    • Before launch each round has a code (hash). After the crash, History shows the round's secret, and sha256(secret:crash) must match the code, so the result can't have been changed.

    Good to know

    • Up to 1,00,000 coins per panel each round.
    • The rocket never goes past 50x: if it gets there, it explodes at exactly 50.00x and anything still in is lost. Cash out before 50x (auto cash out goes up to 49.99x).
    • A bet can't be taken back once placed.
    • If your connection lags, a cash-out counts when the server receives it.
